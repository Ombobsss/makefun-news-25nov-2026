# Wednesday Meridian — 25 November 2026

**Sum up 25 November's world news in one image** — make.fun bounty
`news-image-20261125`.

One broadsheet image, "Wednesday Meridian", that sums up Wednesday
25 November 2026 (UTC): **40 verified stories** — 3 leads, 12 cards,
25 briefs — across 7 regions, with a 6-number strip, a 15-pin world map,
a 9-node UTC timeline and a DATE CHECK box.

## The three leads

1. **US proposes new tariffs on 14 partners: China 20%, EU 15%**
   (Reuters + USTR) — new Section 301 tariffs after an 8-month probe,
   replacing the global tariffs struck down in February 2026.
2. **US economy roars: Q3 GDP revised to 4.3%, but PCE inflation hits 3.8%**
   (Reuters + BEA) — fastest growth since Q3 2023, with inflation at its
   highest since November 2023.
3. **Shenzhou-24: first Pakistani astronaut boards China's Tiangong station**
   (Reuters + CMSA) — Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, first Pakistani in orbit and
   first foreigner aboard Tiangong.

39 stories are marked CONFIRMED (verified to a primary source); exactly
one is marked CLAIM — brief #19, "No proof China told Alice Huang to
spy on Nvidia CEO" (WSJ via Reuters).

## Files
